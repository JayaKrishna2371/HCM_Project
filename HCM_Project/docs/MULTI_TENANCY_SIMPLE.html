Each company has a private floor (a “tenant”). Everyone uses the same front door, but no one can walk onto another company’s floor.

Three kinds of people

🏢

Building Manager

SUPER ADMIN

Runs the whole building. Leases floors to new companies, appoints their managers, can visit any floor.

🧑‍💼

Floor Manager

TENANT ADMIN

Runs one floor — adds or removes their own staff and arranges their rooms. Cannot touch other floors.

👤

Employee

USER

Works on their floor and uses the rooms they’re allowed into. Manages no one.
